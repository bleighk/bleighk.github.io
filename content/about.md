I work at the intersection of strategy and applied AI. The through-line is always the same: complex technology that only creates value when someone can translate it into language a business leader trusts, a process owner can act on, and a team can ship.

My background is in digital strategy and enterprise architecture — seven years across BCG and Deloitte, advising on transformation programmes, technology investment decisions, and the organisational problems that sit upstream of every implementation. Since 2025 I have been at Xero, where I am a strategy ops delegate on the company-wide AI transformation and the connector between Xero's AI engineering capability and its executive layer.

The work I am most proud of is not the strategy decks. It is the things I built when nobody asked me to: the Claude agent that a Xero product team now runs in production, the skills plugin that seeded an internal AI ecosystem, the hackathon prototype that turned a customer churn problem into a live demo. Building is how I stress-test the advice.
